The Personal Consumption Expenditures report showed prices fell more than expected in August. This lower inflation gauge directly impacts Federal Reserve interest rate expectations and moves XAU/USD.

របាយការណ៍ PCE បង្ហាញថាទំនិញបានធ្លាក់ចុះជាងការរំពឹងទុកក្នុងខែសីហា។ ទិន្នន័យនេះប៉ះពាល់ដោយផ្ទាល់ដល់ការរំពឹងទុកអត្រាការប្រាក់របស់ Fed និងរំកិលតម្លៃ XAU/USD។

The latest Personal Consumption Expenditures report, recognized as the Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge, revealed that prices fell more than expected in August. Concurrently, broader economic readings indicated a Consumer Price Index increase of 0.4% in August, an unemployment rate of 4.1%, payroll employment gains of 162,000, and an average hourly earnings rise of $0.10. Additionally, the Producer Price Index for final demand climbed by 0.4%, while the Employment Cost Index recorded a 0.9% increase in the second quarter.

This data release is critical for XAU/USD traders because inflation reports directly dictate Federal Reserve policy outlooks, shaping real yields and the strength of the US dollar. Softer inflation figures typically reduce pressure on the central bank to maintain aggressive monetary tightening, which influences borrowing costs and currency valuations across the board.

ការចេញផ្សាយទិន្នន័យនេះមានសារៈសំខាន់ខ្លាំងណាស់សម្រាប់អ្នកជួញដូរ XAU/USD ព្រោះរបាយការណ៍អតិផរណាបង្ហាញដោយផ្ទាល់នូវទស្សនវិស័យគោលនយោបាយរបស់ Fed ដែលកំណត់ real yields និងកម្លាំងរបស់ដុល្លារអាមេរិក។ តួលេខអតិផរណាដែលធូរស្រាលជាងមុនជាទូទៅកាត់បន្ថយសម្ពាធលើធនាគារកណ្តាលក្នុងការរក្សាការរឹតបន្តឹងផ្នែកម៉ាក្រូសេដ្ឋកិច្ច ដែលមានឥទ្ធិពលលើថ្លៃដើមនៃការខ្ចីប្រាក់និងតម្លៃរូបិយប័ណ្ណទូទាំងទីផ្សារ។

Looking at the potential market dynamics, if cooling inflation figures solidify expectations for looser monetary policy or lower interest rates, the resulting pressure on the US dollar and Treasury yields can provide strong support for XAU/USD. Conversely, if stubborn underlying metrics suggest that price pressures remain persistent, expectations of prolonged high interest rates could strengthen the dollar and weigh heavily on bullion.

Traders should closely monitor upcoming macroeconomic releases, central bank commentary regarding inflation trends, and shifts in employment data to gauge the evolving trajectory of monetary policy. Staying informed on these core indicators will help market participants navigate potential volatility in XAU/USD without relying on short-term price predictions.

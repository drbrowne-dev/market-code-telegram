Gold (XAU/USD) faces a busy calendar of US economic releases that could reshape interest rate expectations. On Thursday, Unemployment Claims are projected at 201K compared to 197K previously, followed later by remarks from FOMC Member Waller and the ISM Manufacturing PMI, which is forecast at 54.8 against the previous 54.6. Friday brings the monthly employment report, featuring Non-Farm Employment Change expected at 89K versus 162K previously, the Unemployment Rate seen steady at 4.1%, and Average Hourly Earnings projected unchanged at 0.3% month-over-month.

តម្លៃមាស (XAU/USD) កំពុងប្រឈមនឹងទិន្នន័យសេដ្ឋកិច្ចអាមេរិកសំខាន់ៗជាច្រើន ដែលអាចជះឥទ្ធិពលដល់ការរំពឹងទុកលើអត្រាការប្រាក់។ នៅថ្ងៃព្រហស្បតិ៍ ទិន្នន័យ Unemployment Claims ត្រូវបានព្យាករណ៍ចំនួន 201K ធៀបនឹង 197K លើកមុន បន្ទាប់មកមានការថ្លែងសុន្ទរកថាពីសមាជិក FOMC លោក Waller និងសន្ទស្សន៍ ISM Manufacturing PMI ដែលរំពឹងថានឹងមានកម្រិត 54.8 ធៀបនឹង 54.6 ពីមុន។ រីឯថ្ងៃសុក្រ របាយការណ៍ការងារសំខាន់រួមមាន Non-Farm Employment Change ត្រូវបានរំពឹងថានឹងមាន 89K ធៀបនឹង 162K កាលពីមុន អត្រា Unemployment Rate រំពឹងថានៅថេរ 4.1% និង Average Hourly Earnings រំពឹងថានៅ 0.3% ដដែល។

These figures are crucial for gold because they directly influence the US dollar and real yields through Federal Reserve policy expectations. As an unyielding asset, bullion is sensitive to shifts in the economic landscape. If the incoming data prints stronger than market forecasts, it could reinforce rate expectations, bolster the dollar, and place downward pressure on gold. Conversely, weaker-than-expected outcomes may weigh on the dollar and Treasury yields, creating potential support for bullion.

Traders should remember that market conditions around major data releases often become disorderly. Volatility frequently surges, and spreads can widen significantly as liquidity adjusts, underscoring the importance of disciplined risk management rather than attempting to predict the exact release figures.

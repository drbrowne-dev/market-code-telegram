Following the release of this employment report, traders rapidly adjusted their expectations regarding monetary policy. Specifically, market participants now see little chance of a Federal Reserve rate increase in October, as the weaker-than-expected data point reduces pressure on the central bank to tighten further.

For XAU/USD traders, employment data serves as a critical transmission channel to gold prices through shifting interest rate expectations and USD dynamics. A cooling labor market typically weighs on expectations for aggressive monetary tightening, which often lowers real yields and pressures the dollar. When yields and the USD lose momentum, non-yielding bullion generally finds renewed demand as investors reassess the macroeconomic landscape.

Traders focusing on XAU/USD should prepare for a significant wave of volatility as the United States releases its latest employment data on Friday at 19:30 Phnom Penh time. The upcoming reports feature Average Hourly Earnings m/m with a consensus forecast of 0.3 percent, matching the previous reading of 0.3 percent. Additionally, the Non-Farm Employment Change is expected to post 89K, compared to the previous reading of 162K, while the Unemployment Rate is forecasted to remain steady at 4.1 percent, matching its prior level.

អ្នកជួញដូរ XAU/USD គួរតែត្រៀមខ្លួនទទួលយកការប្រែប្រួលកម្រិតខ្ពស់ នៅពេលដែលសហរដ្ឋអាមេរិកប្រកាសទិន្នន័យការងារថ្មីបំផុតនៅថ្ងៃសុក្រ វេលាម៉ោង 19:30 នាទី តាមម៉ោងនៅភ្នំពេញ។ ទិន្នន័យដែលត្រូវប្រកាសរួមមាន Average Hourly Earnings m/m ដែលទីផ្សារព្យាករណ៍ទុក 0.3% ធៀបនឹងពេលមុន 0.3%។ ក្រៅពីនេះ Non-Farm Employment Change ត្រូវបានព្យាករណ៍ថានឹងស្ថិតនៅកម្រិត 89K ធៀបនឹងពេលមុន 162K ចំណែកឯ Unemployment Rate ត្រូវបានព្យាករណ៍ថានឹងរក្សាទុកនៅកម្រិត 4.1% ស្មើនឹងពេលមុន។

These high-impact figures are critical for gold traders because labor market health directly influences Federal Reserve rate expectations, real yields, and the strength of the US dollar. A stronger-than-forecast employment report typically supports the USD and yields, which can pressure gold downward. Conversely, a weaker result generally has the opposite effect, softening the dollar and potentially lifting XAU/USD. Market participants should also remember that liquidity can thin and spreads may widen significantly around these major releases, increasing execution risks.
